## Ground 9 — Concession Incorrect: Fixed Trust Misclassification

**The issue:** Revenue NSW applied the special trust rate (2% flat, no threshold) to a unit trust. A unit trust whose beneficiaries are presently entitled to all income is a fixed trust and should be taxed at individual progressive rates, saving approximately $43,484/year.

**What we need to prove:** CASTLE HILL GLEN UNIT TRUST is a unit trust that does NOT meet the statutory definition of "special trust" under LTMA s3A.
